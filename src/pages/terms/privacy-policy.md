This site uses a self-hosted instance of [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to understand how the site is used. The analytics service runs at `stats.050418.xyz` on infrastructure controlled by the site owner.

The service records aggregate information such as the page path and title, referrer, browser and operating-system family, screen width, and approximate country. The country is derived from the visitor's IP address. The IP address and full User-Agent are used temporarily in memory to distinguish visits and are not written to the analytics database.

Normal analytics tracking does not set cookies, use local storage, or create a persistent cross-site identifier. Analytics data is not sold or used for advertising. Requests to this site and the analytics endpoint pass through Cloudflare and the hosting provider, which may process network data under their own privacy policies.

Aggregate statistics are retained until they are no longer needed or are manually deleted. You can disable analytics in this browser by visiting [the opt-out link](https://050418.xyz/#toggle-goatcounter); the page will confirm the change and store the preference locally in your browser.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, please use one of the contact methods on the [About page](/about/).
